Hiding at Sea

New Zealand storm petrels, Hauraki Gulf. Photograph: Duncan (angrysunbird).

Two New Zealand seabirds, written off for more than a century, turned out to have been flying past us the whole time. Finding them meant going out at night, and going out to sea.

Chatham Island tāiko

NamePterodroma magentae, the Magenta petrel
SizeAbout 38 cm long; around 450 g
Lost1867 to 1978
NowFewer than 200 birds; about 20 known breeding burrows

New Zealand storm petrel

NameFregetta maoriana
SizeSparrow-sized; feeds by pattering over the sea
LostAbout 1850 to 2003
NowBreeds on Te Hauturu-o-Toi; first nest site found in 2013

Made, Not Found

Nobody is going to rediscover a moa. So a Texan company has offered to make one. Rediscovery and resurrection sound like the same miracle. They are not, and the difference matters.
